Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026

Summary

CTRUP ("we", "our", "the app") is a personal finance studio designed to keep your data on your device. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal or financial data. We do not require an account, do not perform cloud sync, and do not run third-party analytics or tracking.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

CTRUP is intentionally built without server-side data collection. Specifically, CTRUP does not collect:

  • Personal identification information (name, email, phone, address).
  • Financial account credentials, balances, or transaction details.
  • Investment holdings, broker logins, or portfolio data.
  • Biometric data, contacts, location, or device identifiers.
  • Usage analytics, advertising IDs, or behavioral tracking.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

All data you enter into CTRUP — transactions, accounts, budgets, goals, investments, notes, and receipt photos — is stored locally on your iPhone using Apple's Core Data framework. This data is protected by your device's encryption and is accessible only to CTRUP on your device.

Receipt photos you attach to transactions are saved within the app's local storage and are never uploaded. You may remove these photos at any time by editing or deleting the transaction.

3. Permissions

CTRUP requests only the system permissions necessary for its on-device features:

  • Camera & Photo Library: Used solely to capture or attach receipt photos to your transactions. Photos remain on your device and are never transmitted.
  • Notifications (optional): Used for budget reminders and goal milestones, only if you enable them.

You may decline these permissions and still use all non-photo and non-notification features of the app.

4. No Account, No Login

CTRUP does not require you to create an account. There is no email registration, no social login, and no password. We have no way to identify you or your device from app usage alone.

5. No Cloud Sync, No Backup by Us

Your data is not synced to any server we operate or to any third party. If you enable iCloud device backup on your iPhone, encrypted snapshots of app data may be stored in your personal iCloud backup as part of standard system behavior. This is governed by Apple's iCloud terms, not ours.

6. Analytics & Tracking

CTRUP contains no third-party analytics SDKs, no advertising SDKs, no crash-reporting services, and no tracking pixels. We do not use Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework because there is nothing to track.

7. Data Export & Deletion

You own your data. At any time you can export monthly summaries as CSV from the Reports section, or perform a full data export from Settings. You can also delete any individual record, or remove all app data by deleting the app from your iPhone.

8. Children's Privacy

CTRUP is not directed to children under 13. Because we collect no personal data from anyone, this policy applies equally to all users regardless of age.

9. Third-Party Services

CTRUP integrates with no third-party services that receive your data. Standard Apple frameworks used by the app operate locally on your device.

10. International Users

Because CTRUP does not transmit data, the cross-border data transfer obligations under GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, LGPD, and similar laws do not require action from us. You retain all rights to access, correct, and delete data held locally on your device.

11. Changes to This Policy

If we ever change this policy — for example, to introduce an optional, opt-in feature that transmits data — we will update the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, surface a notice inside the app before the change takes effect.
