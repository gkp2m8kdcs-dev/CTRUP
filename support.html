Frequently Asked Questions

Is CTRUP really private? Does any data leave my phone?

Yes. CTRUP runs entirely on device. There is no account, no cloud sync, and no analytics. All transactions, accounts, budgets, goals, investments, and receipt photos are stored locally on your iPhone. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

Do I need an account or login to use CTRUP?

No. CTRUP is designed to work without any account, login, or registration. Just install and start tracking.

How do I add a transaction?

Open the Transactions tab, tap the "+" button, choose Income or Expense, pick an account and category, enter the amount and date, and optionally attach a receipt photo or note. Save — that's it.

How do budgets with rollover work?

Create a budget from the Budgets tab. Set a period (weekly, monthly, yearly, or custom) and an amount. If you spend less than your budget in a period, the leftover is automatically added to the next period's budget.

Can I track multiple account types?

Yes. CTRUP supports checking, savings, credit, cash, and investment accounts. You can transfer between accounts with automatic balance reconciliation.

How are investment prices updated?

Investment holdings are tracked manually — record buy-ins and update prices as you go. CTRUP shows interactive line charts with selectable time ranges so you can analyze performance over time.

Can I export my data?

Yes. From Settings you can perform a full data export, and from the Reports section you can export monthly summaries as CSV files for sharing with an accountant or for your records.

How do I delete all my data?

You can delete individual records by swiping to delete them, or remove all app data by deleting the CTRUP app from your iPhone.

What iOS version does CTRUP require?

CTRUP requires iOS 17.0 or later on iPhone. It is built natively for iPhone and is not currently available on iPad or Mac.

Tips for the best experience

  • Enable notifications in Settings to receive budget and goal milestone reminders.
  • Attach receipt photos to transactions to keep a paper trail without leaving the app.
  • Use transfers when moving money between accounts so balances stay accurate.
  • Set savings goals with deadlines to give your contributions a target.
  • Export your data periodically from Settings for an extra layer of backup.
